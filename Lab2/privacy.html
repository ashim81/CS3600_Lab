Student portfolio policy

Privacy Policy

This page describes how the forms and external resources work on Asim Sapkota’s student portfolio.

Information Collection

This student portfolio has no database or backend. Its forms do not send or save the details you enter.

Use of Information

The demo forms only show a message on the current page. Submitting a form does not create an account, sign you in, or send a contact message.

Contact Forms

The contact page is for practice and does not deliver messages. Use sample information when trying the form.

Cookies

The project's own code does not set cookies. Embedded services may use cookies or similar technology. See the Cookie Policy for more information.

Third-Party Services

The browser requests Bootstrap files from jsDelivr and a map from Google Maps. These services receive connection information when their resources load.

Contact

My GitHub and LinkedIn profiles are linked on the contact page. The form on that page is a demonstration only.
