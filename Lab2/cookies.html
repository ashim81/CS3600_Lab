What are cookies?

Cookies are small pieces of data a website can ask your browser to store. A website might use them to remember a preference or keep someone signed in.

Cookies in this project

This project's own HTML and JavaScript do not set cookies. The Remember Me checkbox is a visual example and does not store a preference.

External services

The embedded Google Map may use cookies or similar technology controlled by Google. Bootstrap files also load from an external CDN. Review these services and update this sample before publishing.

Your browser choices

Your browser's settings usually let you:

  • View or delete stored cookies.
  • Block some or all cookies.
  • Adjust settings for third-party cookies.

Some external features may work differently when cookies are blocked.
